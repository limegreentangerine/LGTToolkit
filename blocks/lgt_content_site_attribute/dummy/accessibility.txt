<p>At {site_company_name}, we strive to ensure that our website is accessible to all individuals, regardless of their abilities or disabilities. We are committed to providing an inclusive and user-friendly experience for everyone who visits our site. This statement outlines the measures we have taken to make our website accessible and the ongoing efforts we undertake to maintain and improve accessibility.</p>
<h2>WCAG Compliance</h2>
<p>Our website has been designed and developed to comply with the WCAG 2.1 Level AA success criteria. WCAG 2.1 provides recommendations for making web content more accessible to a wide range of disabilities. These guidelines are internationally recognized and widely accepted as the benchmark for web accessibility standards.</p>
<h2>Accessibility Features Implemented</h2>
<ol>
    <li>Perceivable
        <ul>
            <li>We provide alternative text descriptions for all non-text content such as images, icons, and buttons to ensure screen readers can convey the information to visually impaired users.</li>
            <li>We use clear and consistent headings, subheadings, and labels to improve content structure and navigation.</li>
        </ul>
    </li>
    <li>Operable
        <ul>
            <li>Our website can be navigated using a keyboard, ensuring users who cannot operate a mouse can still access all features and functionalities.</li>
            <li>We provide skip navigation links to allow users to bypass repetitive content and navigate directly to the main content area.</li>
            <li>Time-based media, such as videos, have captions or transcripts available to provide equivalent access to audio information.</li>
        </ul>
    </li>
    <li>Understandable
        <ul>
            <li>We strive to use plain and simple language throughout our website, making the content easy to understand for a diverse audience.</li>
            <li>Forms and interactive elements are designed to be clear and straightforward, with appropriate error messages and instructions provided.</li>
        </ul>
    </li>
    <li>Robust
        <ul>
            <li>Our website is built using modern web technologies and follows best practices to ensure compatibility with current and future assistive technologies.</li>
        </ul>
    </li>
</ol>
